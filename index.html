1Information we collect

NoteKhana does not collect any personal information. The developer cannot see, receive or access anything you type or save in the app.

The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details.

2How your data is stored

The following information is created by you while using the app and is stored locally on your device only:

  • Notes: titles, text, category, color, pin status and timestamps, saved in a local database inside the app's private storage.
  • Settings: your theme choice (light, dark or system) and default note color, saved in the app's private preferences.

This data is never uploaded, synced or transmitted anywhere. App data is excluded from cloud backup, so it exists only on the device where you created it.

4Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data. NoteKhana does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social media SDKs, and it does not connect to any server.

5Ads and tracking

NoteKhana contains no advertisements. It does not track you, build a profile about you, or use advertising identifiers or cookies.

6Your control and deletion

  • You can edit or delete any note inside the app at any time.
  • You can clear all data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us, and nothing remains with us after you delete it.

7Security

Your notes are kept in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot normally access on a standard, non-rooted Android device. Because the data never leaves your device, its safety also depends on how you protect your device, for example with a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device protected.

8Children's privacy

NoteKhana does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app collects no personal data from any user.

9Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
